Tap BPM

Tap along to a beat to find its tempo.

— BPM

Tap the button or press Space to begin.

You can also press the Space key.

How it works

Tap in time with the music. After two taps, your BPM appears. Keep tapping for a steadier reading based on the most recent beats. Pause for five seconds to start a new measurement, or choose Reset at any time.

Convert kilobytes to megabytes instantly. Enter any value to see the result.

Kilobytes (KB)→Megabytes (MB)

Conversion formula

Multiply KB by 0.001 to get MB.

Decimal units use SI: 1 KB = 1,000 B, 1 MB = 1,000,000 B. Binary units use IEC: 1 KiB = 1,024 B, 1 MiB = 1,048,576 B.

Examples

  • 1 KB = 0.001 MB
  • 10 KB = 0.01 MB

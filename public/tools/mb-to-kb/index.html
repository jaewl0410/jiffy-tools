Megabytes (MB)→Kilobytes (KB)

Conversion formula

Multiply MB by 1,000 to get KB.

Decimal units use SI: 1 KB = 1,000 B, 1 MB = 1,000,000 B. Binary units use IEC: 1 KiB = 1,024 B, 1 MiB = 1,048,576 B.

Examples

  • 1 MB = 1,000 KB
  • 10 MB = 10,000 KB

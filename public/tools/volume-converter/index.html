Volume Converter

Convert volume units instantly. Choose two units, enter a value, and see the result as you type.

How to use it

Choose any two volume units. The result updates as you type, and Swap reverses the direction.

Gallons, fluid ounces, cups, and pints here use US customary units.

Examples

  • 1 L = 1,000 mL
  • 10 L = 10,000 mL

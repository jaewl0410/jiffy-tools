Conversion formula

Multiply GB by 1,000 to get MB.

Decimal units use SI: 1 KB = 1,000 B, 1 MB = 1,000,000 B. Binary units use IEC: 1 KiB = 1,024 B, 1 MiB = 1,048,576 B.

These pair results use decimal KB, MB, and GB (1,000 per step). For 1,024-based units, choose KiB, MiB, or GiB in the Data Storage Converter.

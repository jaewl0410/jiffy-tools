Frequency Converter

Convert frequency units instantly. Choose two units, enter a value, and see the result as you type.

How to use it

Choose any two frequency units. The result updates as you type, and Swap reverses the direction.

RPM means cycles or revolutions per minute; 60 RPM equals 1 Hz.

Examples

  • 1 Hz = 0.001 kHz
  • 10 Hz = 0.01 kHz

Power Converter

Convert power units instantly. Choose two units, enter a value, and see the result as you type.

How to use it

Choose any two power units. The result updates as you type, and Swap reverses the direction.

Examples

  • 1 W = 0.001 kW
  • 10 W = 0.01 kW

Privacy Policy

Jiffy does not require an account. The current tools run in your browser; image files and other tool inputs are not sent to Jiffy for processing. A future tool that needs an upload will say so on its page.

Hosting and analytics

Cloudflare hosts this site and may process basic request information, such as IP address, browser details, and pages requested, to serve and protect it. Usage analytics are not active because no measurement ID is configured. The site code does not load a separate analytics service or set analytics or advertising cookies.

Advertising and cookies

We may add ads later. If we use Google AdSense or another ad provider, we will update this policy when advertising is enabled.

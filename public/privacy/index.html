Privacy Policy

Jiffy does not require an account. The current tools run in your browser; image files and other tool inputs are not sent to Jiffy for processing. A future tool that needs an upload will say so on its page.

Hosting and analytics

Cloudflare hosts this site and may process basic request information, such as IP address, browser details, and pages requested, to serve and protect it. We use Google Analytics 4 to measure page views and tool usage. Our custom events contain only a tool identifier, category, and page path; we do not send tool inputs, results, file names, or file contents as event parameters. Google may process request information, including IP address and browser details, when these events are sent. We configure Google Analytics to deny analytics and advertising cookie storage. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Advertising and cookies

We intend to use Google AdSense, but Jiffy does not currently display ads. If advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies for ad delivery and measurement. Before ads are shown, we will update this policy with the active advertising practices and provide any required consent choices.
